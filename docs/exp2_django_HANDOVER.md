## Current findings (the final list is the Findings section of `results/exp2_results_tables.md`: 8 from 1 Nov, plus Finding 9, arm H0, owner sign-off 5 Nov, which raised the cap to 9; items below are the working notes)
1. No arm or prompt difference survives Holm in the primary 60-test family (smallest Holm p 0.76: 4B F0/v3 vs S0/v3 overlap-cited 14/3, p=0.013). Caveat: 50 questions; contain-cited gives 12/4, p=0.077.
2. 4B S-0.25/v3 is overlap-cited on 7 questions where S0-G/v3 is not and never the reverse (0/7, p=0.016; contain-cited 0/7, Holm p 0.31 in the 20-test family). Caveat: S-0.25/v3 also answers more unanswerables (5 vs 2 of 17).
3. 8B answers more unanswerables than 4B in every arm and prompt: 11-14 of 17 vs 2-7 (15-row sensitivity: 9-12 vs 2-5). Caveat: 17 questions, counts only.
4. v3 lowers 4B's unanswerable answers in every arm (7->3, 7->4, 6->2, 7->5; b/c 2-4/0, unadjusted p 0.125-0.5); 8B changes by 0-2. Caveat: no single test reaches p < 0.05.
5. Sentence-level support (MiniCheck cited min, mean per stratum) ranges 0.36-0.68 and is highest for F0 in every model x prompt. Caveat: support proxy, not the right reason; cited and full premises are not a paired comparison.
6. Gate at 0.5 keeps 301/659 answered answerables, 229/453 overlap-cited answers and 59/141 unanswerable answers (15 of the 59 from nr9172 and nr13883). Caveat: threshold set in planning, rule file committed after the faithfulness results (P1).
7. Against all answered answerables, MiniCheck AUROC is 0.54 [0.44, 0.66] (4B) and 0.57 [0.47, 0.67] (8B); HHEM 0.67 [0.56, 0.78] and 0.73 [0.62, 0.82]. Caveat: positives from 9 (4B) and 14 (8B) PR families; PR-family bootstrap.
8. Correctness (Codex labels, 1 Nov, `results/correctness_test_arms.csv`): strict 19-30 of 50 (4B), 21-27 (8B); lenient 29-38 (4B), 34-40 (8B). No correctness McNemar test reaches p < 0.05 (0 of 40; smallest p 0.070, 4B S0-G/v2 vs S-0.25/v2 strict 1/7; all Holm p = 1.0), so no arm or prompt is ranked on correctness. Caveat: 50 questions; counts only.
9. Correctness AUROC (`results/gate_auroc_correctness.csv`): unanswerable answers vs judged-correct answers, HHEM 0.70 [0.58, 0.83] (4B) and 0.78 [0.65, 0.87] (8B); MiniCheck 0.57 [0.46, 0.70] and 0.61 [0.49, 0.73]. Caveat: same PR-family bootstrap and small positive families as finding 7.
