## Findings

1. **No arm or prompt difference survives Holm in its family** (smallest Holm p: primary 60 0.7635, contain 20 0.3125, correctness 40 1.0). Differences between arms are counts only. *Caveat:* 50 answerable questions; a few questions either way is noise.
2. **8B answers more unanswerable questions than 4B** in every arm and prompt: 11-14 of 17 vs 2-7 (15-question sensitivity: 9-12 vs 2-5). *Caveat:* 17 questions, counts only, no test.
3. **Prompt v3 lowers 4B's unanswerable answers in every arm** (F0 7->3, S0 7->4, S0-G 6->2, S-0.25 7->5); 8B changes by 0-2. *Caveat:* unadjusted p 0.125-0.5; no single test reaches p < 0.05.
4. **Correctness (Codex):** strict 19-30 of 50 (4B) and 21-27 (8B); lenient 29-38 and 34-40. Codex vs Alyssa (n = 80): kappa 0.675 [0.511, 0.816]. *Caveat:* counts only, no Holm survivor in the correctness 40 family; one LLM judge checked by one human rater. Codex is stricter than Alyssa on correct vs partial (9 of 13 disagreements), so strict rates may run low; lenient is less affected.
5. **Sentence-level support** (MiniCheck cited min, mean per stratum) ranges 0.36-0.68 and is highest for F0 in every model x prompt. *Caveat:* a support proxy, not the right reason; no test. Fixed cited chunks are longer than structural ones (mean context chunk 396 vs 92 Exp 1 units), so support is not comparable across chunkings.
6. **Gate at 0.5** keeps 301/659 answered answerables, 229/453 overlap-cited answers and 59/141 unanswerable answers (15 of them from nr9172 and nr13883). *Caveat:* threshold set in planning, rule file committed after the faithfulness results (P1).
7. **Discrimination (AUROC, unanswerable answers as positives):** vs all answered answerables, MiniCheck 0.538 [0.437, 0.656] (4B) and 0.572 [0.468, 0.673] (8B), HHEM 0.669 [0.563, 0.784] and 0.732 [0.622, 0.824]; vs judged-correct answers, HHEM 0.698 [0.583, 0.825] and 0.775 [0.646, 0.873], MiniCheck 0.572 [0.46, 0.7] and 0.61 [0.485, 0.725]. *Caveat:* PR-family bootstrap with few positive families; HHEM above MiniCheck is a point-estimate difference with overlapping CIs and no paired test; the gate keeps MiniCheck (test-set result, no rule change).
8. **Arm W-F (exploratory):** whole-PR packing has the answer span in context on 10 fewer questions than F0 (32 vs 42 of 50). Of the 11 questions where F0 has it and W-F does not, 6 have a gold PR over the 2,560 budget (3 truncated at rank 1, 3 skipped) and 5 have a smaller gold PR skipped because higher-ranked PRs filled the budget. On the 31 questions with the answer span in context for both, W-F was correct on 1-2 more questions than F0 under lenient labels, and 1-6 more under strict labels, which cross-session label drift can affect. No pre-declared W-F vs F0 test reaches p < 0.05. *Caveat:* exploratory arm proposed after the test results; descriptive subset.
9. **Arm H0 (hybrid; proposed after the frozen results):** gold PR in the top 5 PRs 47 vs 43 of 50, with no sign of the Exp 1 drop. No evidence of a reduction in hallucination (unanswerable answered 7->8, 3->2, 13->12, 13->11). Strict correct rises in all four cells, but no test survives Holm in the hybrid 16 family (smallest Holm p 0.118). *Caveat:* 50 + 17 questions; H0 labels come from a later Codex session and have no human validation.

## 9. Limitations

- **Cited vs full-context premises are not a paired estimate (P6).** Each claim's MiniCheck score is
  its maximum over windows. Full contexts make more windows than cited contexts, so they get more
  chances at a high maximum, while support that spans separated chunks can be split across windows;
  HHEM sees one long premise. In the full run cited minus full > 0.1 in 169/658 (MiniCheck) and
  126/658 (HHEM) rows. Cited and full scores are reported as two premise conditions, cited primary;
  no context-benefit claim is made from their difference.
- **The faithfulness measure is sentence-level support (P7).** MiniCheck and HHEM score whether each
  answer sentence is supported by the premise. A sentence can be supported by the PR without being
  the reason for the change, and an omitted or wrong causal link is not detected. Correctness
  (right reason) is judged separately (blind Codex labels, Alyssa's validation sample).
- **Sentence extraction is rule-based (P8).** Answers are split on `.`, `!` or `?` followed by
  whitespace, so abbreviations such as "e.g." can split a sentence, and fragments under 3 words are
  dropped. Either can change the minimum sentence score the gate uses; the effect on gate decisions
  has not been measured.
- **Correctness judge.** Correctness labels are from one LLM judge (Codex, blind to model, arm and
  prompt; citations removed), primary for every correctness table and test. They are checked
  against one independent human rater (Alyssa, reference; blind to Codex labels, model and
  condition) on n = 80 rows (10 per model x prompt x chunking stratum): 3-label Cohen's kappa
  0.675 [0.511, 0.816], strict (correct vs rest) 0.692 [0.525, 0.845], lenient (correct or partial
  vs wrong) 0.772 [0.481, 0.953]. Codex is stricter on correct vs partial (9 of the 13
  disagreements: Alyssa correct, Codex partial); Alyssa is stricter on wrong (3 of 13: Alyssa
  wrong, Codex correct or partial; the reverse, Alyssa partial / Codex wrong, 1). Strict
  correctness may therefore run low compared with a human judge. The lenient kappa CI is wide
  because Alyssa labelled only 11 rows wrong. One human rater and 80 rows; there is no second
  human rater.
- **Same-ticket PRs in the pool.** 10 of the 50 answerable gold PRs have a same-ticket PR in pool A
  (Exp 1's same-ticket filter covered its fixing PRs only). A same-ticket PR may legitimately contain
  the reason, so citing one is not always borrowing. Codex judged against the gold only (q15435:
  partial). The pool is frozen; the effect is seen on 1 question, 2/800 answered rows.
- **Arm W-F is exploratory.** It was proposed after the test results were seen
  (`tuning_rule_wholepr.md`), sits outside every Holm family, and its comparisons carry unadjusted p
  only. Its ceiling is lower: answer span in context 32/50 vs 42 (F0) and 43-44 (structural
  arms), because only the rank-1 PR may be truncated and gold PRs over 2,560 units at lower ranks are
  skipped. W faithfulness is not comparable with the chunk arms: whole-PR premises are longer, so
  MiniCheck takes the max over more windows and HHEM reads past 512 tokens. 8B's chunk-style citations
  (`[PR<n>-c<k>]`, 88 of 99 8B citations) are a prompt-format artefact (the frozen prompts' example
  id); they count as PR n.
- **Cross-session Codex drift (W-F labels).** The W-F rows were labelled in a later Codex session. 20 old
  ids re-judged in that session: 17/20 agree (kappa 0.76); all 3 changes are partial -> correct
  (J0031, J0096, J0433). Strict W-F counts may therefore be slightly lenient compared with the frozen arms. One
  duplicate answer went the other way (J0652 frozen, correct; J0715 W-F, partial). Lenient counts are
  unaffected by partial/correct changes. Drift labels are not used in any analysis.
- **Arm H0 was proposed after the frozen results.** It has its own Holm family (hybrid 16) and changes
  no frozen arm. Its rows were labelled in a later Codex session than F0 and have no human validation.
  20 old ids re-judged in that session: 18/20 agree (kappa 0.78); changes: J0218 partial -> correct; J0345 correct -> partial. 3 duplicate-answer
  groups that include an H0 row got different labels (J0068, J0072, J0217, J0396, J0711, J0967; J0086, J0322, J0584, J0602, J0934; J0736, J0804); they are reported and not relabelled.
  Power is low: surviving Holm takes at least 10 discordant pairs all one way (stated in
  `tuning_rule_hybrid.md` before any data), so a non-significant result is not evidence of no effect.
- **SymPy is a small transfer check, not a replication.** 15 answerable and 7 unanswerable questions, one model
  and prompt, no tests, and no hybrid arm or gate output in its Exp 2. Its labels are from two human raters
  (lower label kept), while Django's are from Codex, which was stricter than Alyssa on correct vs partial, so
  strict counts are not like-for-like across projects. All 4 rater disagreements are on S0-G, so the F0 vs
  S0-G strict order depends on the merge rule. It ran on a different GPU from the Django runs.
- **Curated question set (P9).** 50 answerable test questions, one per shortlisted PR, with
  questions and gold answers written by Claude and reviewed by James and Codex. Verbatim span checks
  show the quotes exist; they do not show that every clause of each gold answer is needed or fully
  supported. The set favours PRs with an explicit, rationale-rich discussion and is not a
  representative sample of Django PRs.
- **Gate threshold timing (P1).** Threshold 0.5 specified in planning before the full faithfulness
  run (only a 30-row check existed). The rule file was committed after
  results/summary_faithfulness.csv (2da7d48 before 074812c). The sweep (0.1-0.9) is shown; 0.5 is
  not presented as a confirmatory result.
