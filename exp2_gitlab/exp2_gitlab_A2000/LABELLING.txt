Labelling the GitLab Runner correctness sheet (same rules as Exp 2's judging/codex_instructions.md)

Each row is one model answer to a "why" question about a GitLab Runner merge request. You see the question,
the gold answer, the gold quotes (verbatim MR text that states the reason) and the model answer.
The arm is hidden and citation ids were removed. Do not open exp2_gitlab_A2000/keys/.

Fill `label` with exactly one of: correct / partial / wrong, and give a one-line `reason`.
- correct: states the reason the gold quotes support (paraphrase is fine; extra details are fine
  only if they do not contradict the MR).
- partial: gets part of the reason, or is in the right area but misses the key point.
- wrong: a different or invented reason, or contradicts the gold.
Judge against the gold quotes, not against general knowledge. Do not change any other column.

There is one row per arm and question. Two rows can show the same question, or even the same answer: they are
different arms, so label every row, and never mark one as a duplicate.
